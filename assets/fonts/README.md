# Bundled fonts

## ChicagoFLF.ttf

The Mac OS 9 window-title, menu-bar and heading face. Chicago itself is
Apple's; this is the public-domain recreation by Robin Casady, placed in the
public domain by the author (email, 2014-01-30, shipped in the original
distribution):

> The FLF were created by various authors including myself and Mike Wright.
> When Casady & Greene, Inc. closed the rights went back to the authors. I
> created ChicagoFLF and CheckboxFLF. You are free to use them as you wish. I
> consider those two in the public domain.

Source: <https://fontlibrary.org/en/font/chicagoflf> (v2.0, 2014).
No modifications. Body text uses the Geneva/Helvetica system stack: no
free recreation of Geneva is bundled.
